## What the council is

A standing group of practicing educators and specialists who co-design, pressure-test, and evidence every module before it reaches a classroom. The implementation plan budgets **8–15 aggregate hours weekly** across the whole council — individual members contribute as little as 30–60 minutes a week. The council holds real gates: no module ships without its usability and fidelity evidence, and no single role's gate can be waived by another (GOVERNANCE.md).

## What participation looks like

- **Think-aloud sessions** (30–45 min, recorded notes only, never audio/video by default): the educator uses a build while narrating; we measure time-to-artifact, edit burden, and where trust breaks.
- **Fixture review, and later authoring by separate assent**: rating project-supplied synthetic classroom materials is council work. Member-authored fixtures remain outside the repository and distribution artifacts until the project chooses its content license and that author separately accepts the applicable contribution terms. **Never student work**; the absolute rule of CONTRIBUTING.md applies throughout.
- **Co-design reviews**: reading a module spec against real classroom practice before code exists.
- **Seeded-error studies** (later): catching planted defects, which gates release (plan §12).
