# Educator council — charter and recruitment kit

**Status:** Charter ready; recruitment is a human act only the typist can perform. This document makes each invitation a five-minute task.

## What the council is

A standing group of practicing educators and specialists who co-design, pressure-test, and evidence every module before it reaches a classroom. The implementation plan budgets **8–15 aggregate hours weekly** across the whole council — individual members contribute as little as 30–60 minutes a week. The council holds real gates: no module ships without its usability and fidelity evidence, and no single role's gate can be waived by another (GOVERNANCE.md).

## What participation looks like

- **Think-aloud sessions** (30–45 min, recorded notes only, never audio/video by default): the educator uses a build while narrating; we measure time-to-artifact, edit burden, and where trust breaks.
- **Fixture authoring and review**: creating and rating synthetic classroom materials — **never student work**; the one absolute rule of CONTRIBUTING.md applies to council materials too.
- **Co-design reviews**: reading a module spec against real classroom practice before code exists.
- **Seeded-error studies** (later): catching planted defects, which gates release (plan §12).

## Ethics and boundaries

Council members are advisors, not test subjects: no student data is ever solicited; sessions use staged or synthetic materials; findings are recorded de-identified; members may withdraw at any time and are credited in release notes unless they prefer otherwise.

## Decision the typist owns

**Recognition and compensation policy** — whether council seats are volunteer (credited), honoraria-supported, or district-time (if the district partnership formalizes). Recommendation: decide before the first invitation is sent, and say it plainly in the invitation; specialist time (SLP, AT reviewer) most deserves compensation if any budget exists.
