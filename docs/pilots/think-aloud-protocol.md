# Think-aloud protocol — facilitator kit

> **NOT READY FOR PARTICIPANT USE.** The script below contains proposed consent,
> withdrawal-right, credit, and private-record language that has not been
> enacted. The typist must issue and approve one exact corrective proposal, the
> initial cohort must validly enact that same version, and the private custodian must record each applicable
> choice and separately explain the withdrawal right and route before scheduling a session
> or collecting any note, recording, quotation, or participant-authored material.

**For:** H7 teacher-pilot review, only after H0–H6 records are frozen, every predecessor hold applicable to the exact H7 build/instrument/material is explicitly closed by its accountable authority, and the controlling [bounded-commission review ledger's entry conditions](../council/bounded-commission-review-ledger.md#entry-conditions) are satisfied; the [coordination plan](human-gates-coordination-plan.md) remains the operational companion · **Session length:** 30–45 minutes · **Recorded:** notes only

A think-aloud is not a demo and not a test of the teacher. The teacher works; the software is on trial. The facilitator's discipline is silence: every hint given is a defect hidden.

- [ ] The task card for this session printed (below). One task per session.
- [ ] Note sheet ready (template at bottom). A visible clock or timer.
- [ ] The participation terms read aloud or handed over (next section); participation, private/de-identified note collection, public-record publication, recording, public credit, and compensation election recorded separately; every refusal honored; the withdrawal right and route separately explained and acknowledged. The private compensation ledger records dated counsel/accountant reverification, an authorized disbursement route, the exact elected rate, quarter-to-date commitments, and enough remaining cap for the full rate before scheduling; a decline is zero and non-retroactive. Exact participant wording remains private while the content license and contribution-assent route are unresolved.
- [ ] The participant told when the proposed de-identified record will be available for review before it is committed or otherwise published.

## Participation terms (read to the participant)

> This session evaluates the software, not you — there are no wrong moves, and the places you get stuck are the most valuable thing you can give us. I'll take written notes. Audio or video requires a separate choice and is off by default. Everything is synthetic — no student names, no student work, ever, including in examples you improvise. You may stop now or end future participation and collection at any time. The lawful-payment route was reverified before this session. If you separately elected compensation, the full rate disclosed to you fits your remaining quarterly cap and attaches once this scheduled session begins; stopping or withdrawing does not forfeit it. If you declined compensation, the amount is zero and will not become retroactive. Before the proposed de-identified notes are committed or otherwise published, you may review them and ask that your material be corrected or removed. A public Git history or an artifact already copied by others cannot honestly be promised erased; a later correction, withdrawal marker, or credit change applies to the current record and future use. Public credit is a separate opt-in choice. Participation, compensation, note collection, and public-record publication consent are not permission to publish your exact words or contribute a fixture, translation, illustration, or other authored material. Exact wording and authored material stay with the private custodian unless the project first chooses the applicable content license and you separately assent to those exact contribution terms.

## Running the session

1. **Set the task** (hand over the card, start the timer): e.g., *"Make a task strip for a classroom routine you actually run, using made-up details."* or *"Produce a 5 mm graph-paper booklet, two signatures."*
2. **Ask them to narrate**: what they're looking for, what they expect a control to do, what surprised them.
3. **Say nothing that helps.** Permitted utterances: "What are you thinking?", "What did you expect to happen?", "Say more?" If they are stuck ≥2 minutes, note the stall (that's the finding), then unstick them with the *minimum* hint and mark the task **assisted**.
4. **Stop the timer** at the moment of approved artifact (or paper in hand, for Press tasks).
5. **Debrief (5 min)**: Would this earn a place in your week? What would you not trust it with? What did the review screen make you feel — checked, or checked-up-on?

## What to measure (plan §17 human-value measures)

| Measure | How |
|---|---|
| Time-to-artifact | Timer, task start → approval/print. Compare against the recipe's stated budget (constitution item 14: a recipe that can't meet its budget is redesigned, not excused) |
| Edit burden | Count of corrections the teacher made in review before approving |
| Stalls | Each ≥2 min stall: where, and what they said |
| Assisted? | Yes/no + the hint given |
| Trust breaks | Participant-reviewed de-identified factual paraphrase of the moment they hesitated to rely on the output; exact words remain private unless the content license is chosen, the author separately assents to those exact contribution terms, and separate public-record publication consent covers that wording |
