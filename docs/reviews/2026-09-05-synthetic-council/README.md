# Da Vinci's synthetic educator council — sessions 13–20

**Prepared:** 5 September 2026. **Status:** completed synthetic advisory review.
**Evidence baseline:** `965a59abb80a4f1671f34d13ee34f82cb7f5a624`.
**Authorship:** one AI assistant applying the explicitly fictional lenses below.
These are structured deliberations written for this commission, not transcripts,
independent reviewers, human meetings, votes, appointments, or H0–H7 records.
Da Vinci is a design persona, not Leonardo da Vinci speaking or a claim of
professional qualification. All proposed rulings govern this advisory report
only; product adoption follows the existing human and ADR routes.

The council's central recommendation is to make Honest Ink a complete instrument
for the cycle of teaching: establish a worthwhile target, understand its demands,
prepare evidence and materials, explain and model, offer ways to participate,
notice understanding, respond, support revision and transfer, and improve the
next lesson. A larger list of printable products alone will not accomplish that.
The software should make those relationships inspectable and editable while
reducing the teacher's production work.

The current engine provides a strong base for that ambition. The present gap is
between mechanically valid artifacts and independently demonstrated instructional
value, access, usability, and lasting reuse. The recommendation is to improve
those connections and prove them on the existing surfaces before selecting more
atlas products. The real needs-first council must remain free to reject that
recommendation and identify needs this synthetic exercise cannot know.

## The synthetic master educator

**Name:** The Master Educator, a fictional integrator chaired by Da Vinci.
**Purpose:** expose and improve the relationship between teacher intention,
learner intellectual work, evidence, access, material constraints, and revision.
**Operating method:** observe → distinguish → design alternatives → rehearse a
counterexample → specify proof → recommend → revisit after human evidence.

The persona carries twelve explicit habits. Each has a visible work product,
so “virtuosity” does not become an untestable adjective.

| Habit | Work product and failure question |
|---|---|
| Discern the target | Target, construct, and success evidence; what intellectual work would an easier-looking version remove? |
| Know the discipline | Source and subject model; what would a subject expert dispute? |
| Learn the context | Teacher-stated time, materials, language, and access constraints; which assumption is unverified? |
| Make thought visible | Model, example, nonexample, and explanation; is an answer being leaked into independent work? |
| Invite participation | Meaningful options, wait, pass, help, and repair; is any option merely decorative? |
| Anticipate misunderstanding | Competing plausible reasoning and a discriminating check; are we confusing missing evidence with error? |
| Respond proportionately | A small next move linked to evidence; can the teacher reject it without rebuilding the lesson? |
| Scaffold and release | A removable support and observable transfer condition; does the support become permanent by accident? |
| Rehearse the room | Transitions, preparation, print handling, and contingencies; does it work when the copier or internet fails? |
| Preserve dignity | Non-deficit language and no forced disclosure; does convenience create coercion or surveillance? |
| Steward the commons | Editable source, provenance, attribution, compatibility, and repair instructions; can another teacher continue it? |
| Revise the judgment | Counterevidence, dissent, uncertainty, and a revisit trigger; what would change the recommendation? |

The persona cannot assess a real learner, infer an individual need, authenticate
an expert, or confer credentials. Its output is a tool for the accountable human
teacher described in the constitution, not a new runtime “master teacher.”

## Council lenses and deliberation rule

| Fictional lens | Question it must press | Limitation it must disclose |
|---|---|---|
| Master educator | Does the complete lesson preserve worthwhile intellectual work? | No classroom observation or professional identity |
| Early learning and literacy | Are language, play, decoding, meaning, and development distinguished? | No claim of universal method or diagnosis |
| Mathematics and measurement | Are representations, units, conditions, and inferences valid? | Arithmetic checks alone do not establish instruction |
| Science, making, arts, and movement | Do investigation, creativity, embodied practice, and safe preparation survive? | Cannot author or approve hazardous procedures |
| Humanities and civic inquiry | Are sources, uncertainty, power, and disagreement treated honestly? | Cannot invent quotation, historical context, or community voice |
| Agency and access | Can the teacher and learner participate, decline, repair, and use their own modes? | Does not occupy AAC/SLP or AT seats |
| Multilingual and family partnership | Is meaning preserved and communication actionable? | No authenticated language-pair or interpreter qualification |
| Classroom enactment | What happens at preparation, transition, interruption, and cleanup? | No measured teacher workload or physical print experience |
| Rights, privacy, and governance | Who may decide, use, retain, share, and withdraw what? | No consent, legal determination, or district authority |
| Engine and continuity | Can the exact artifact be reviewed, reproduced, recovered, and maintained? | Code inspection is not independent security or release certification |

No votes or quorum are counted. A session records evidence, a competing argument,
the reasoned recommendation, residual disagreement, and what would overturn it.
All lenses are authored by the same assistant; their agreement is not independent
corroboration. There are no invented quotations, participant biographies, school
stories, test scores, or observations of real people.

## Session 13 — Constitution, evidence, and the meaning of excellence

**Question:** What should a synthetic master educator be allowed to conclude?

The governance lens brings the 14 constitutional principles, the 44 open
stage-gate criteria, the unrun H0–H7 sequence, and the previous rehearsal records.
The engine lens brings current hosted success and the distinction between 336
structural instructional tests and independent teaching-quality ratings.
The educator lens objects to treating a green build as a curriculum verdict.
The continuity lens also objects to dismissing useful engine proof because
human evidence is absent: exact approval, bounded output, and reversible storage
are necessary foundations worth retaining.

**Disposition D13:** retain the foundation; describe every judgment by its
evidence class. This report may recommend improvements and expose contradictions.
It may not fill a human decision field or present generated role dialogue as
consensus. Excellence means a teachable, inspectable, adaptable program whose
failures can be found and repaired. “For all time” is a maintenance and portability
obligation, not a prediction that software or pedagogy can become infallible.

**Proposals:** I01–I04 and I34. **Counterposition retained:** governance paperwork
can itself become a barrier. I02 calls for one concise decision index pointing
to authoritative records, without copying private participant records into Git.
**Revisit when:** a constituted council identifies a burden or an authority rule
requires amendment; amendment must be explicit rather than an informal bypass.

## Session 14 — The whole educational program

**Question:** Does coverage of 227 ideas cover a teacher's work?

The curriculum lens inventories all 25 studios and the 13 starting modules,
including Module Zero. Planning, routines, formative checks, revision, libraries,
arts, CTE, family communication, professional learning, and operations are all
present in the atlas. Presence is a possibility, not implementation. The educator
lens finds a missing organizing relationship: an artifact can exist without
showing which target it serves, what learners will do, what evidence it elicits,
or which teaching response follows.

The engine lens proposes a universal wizard. The classroom lens challenges the
entry burden and the assumption that every blank graph-paper sheet needs a
complete lesson plan. The resulting design uses a small optional lesson brief
for compositions and a direct route for simple production. Purpose and review
remain explicit; repeated typing should not be the price of coherence.

**Disposition D14:** recommend a common teaching-cycle contract and reusable,
teacher-controlled links among approved components, not 227 independent apps.
All 227 entries receive a disposition in the companion map. New compositions
remain hypotheses for H0, with an option to choose none. The map is deliberately
unranked; the commission order later in this report ranks dependencies and
repairs, not the real council's educational priorities.

**Proposals:** I05–I10, I18, I22–I25, I38. **Counterposition retained:** a common
contract could flatten subject differences. Every subject section therefore
names an invariant a generic template cannot establish. **Revisit when:** a
teacher cannot express a valid practice without distorting it to fit the model.

## Session 15 — Instruction, subject knowledge, and evidence of learning

**Question:** What must improve beyond nonblank fields and valid geometry?

The source review of `ScaffoldSmith.cs` confirms that rationale fields are
required, but meaningful target preservation and freedom from answer leakage
are not established by those checks. `TalkMoves.cs` requires question purposes,
evidence targets, distinct participation labels, and five move families; it has
no time parameter or sensitive-topic decision record. `SourceLens.cs` preserves
an excerpt and formats metadata, but a nonempty corroboration list cannot prove
that the supplied sources corroborate anything. These match the open P-03/P-04
rows rather than new claims of diagnosed exploit paths.

The mathematics lens recommends deterministic checking wherever a quantity or
relation has a formal meaning. The humanities lens resists applying the same
model to interpretation: several supported readings can be valid. The literacy
lens separates decoding, vocabulary, fluent reading, comprehension, and writing
so that sentence simplification is not mistaken for all literacy teaching.

**Disposition D15:** combine structural checks with subject-specific reference
models, contrasting examples, source pointers, and independent review. Preserve
an unresolved category. Use bounded teacher-authored materials for rehearsal.
Learning-science sources inform choices without proving Honest Ink effective:
the [IES study guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1) supports examining
spacing, retrieval, representations, examples, and explanation, with differing
evidence levels; the [IES foundational-reading guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/21/Published)
has a K–3 scope and does not validate every age, language, or script.

**Proposals:** I07–I16, I23–I25, I39. **Counterposition retained:** additional
validation may reject legitimate unconventional work. Acceptance requires valid
counterexamples and a teacher-visible explanation, not a keyword blacklist.
**Revisit when:** reviewers disagree about the target or a counterexample defeats
the current constraint; preserve that disagreement in the criterion record.

## Session 16 — Agency, access, language, and family partnership

**Question:** Can an attractive artifact exclude someone or change the task?

The access lens traces the separate UI-language and artifact-language contracts.
English artifact furniture, installed-system-font dependence, and the native
PDF's bounded character repertoire remain current limitations. The multilingual
lens notes that exact dates and aligned rows cannot prove translated meaning.
The agency lens questions the existing task-entry default that suggests removing
the card after a learner begins within 30 seconds: initiation speed alone does
not establish independent transfer, and a learner may still choose a useful aid.

The productivity lens wants ready-made symbol-rich defaults. The agency lens
requires the option of text, a different representation, or no symbol, and real
AAC/SLP review of meanings and use. A fictional lens cannot stand in for that
experience. [CAST UDL 3.0](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/) informs the proposal
to design choices across engagement, representation, and expression; it does
not certify a preset or establish an individual's access requirements.

**Disposition D16:** treat access, language, and learner agency as explicit design
and review dimensions of every artifact. Preserve the intellectual demand while
changing avoidable barriers. The [W3C meaningful-sequence explanation](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/meaningful-sequence)
supports preserving programmatically available reading order; HTML structure
still needs real assistive-technology testing on the actual outputs.

**Proposals:** I10, I15–I21, I32, I39. **Counterposition retained:** too many choices
can make authoring difficult. Use reviewed defaults with visible overrides and
progressive disclosure; do not force one representation on every learner.
**Revisit when:** H1, H3, or H4 rejects a meaning, translation, focus path, or
default, or a participant cannot complete the task with their chosen mode.

## Session 17 — The lesson as enacted, including when things fail

**Question:** Does a correct page become a workable lesson at the classroom door?

The classroom lens examines preparation, bell-to-bell timing, transitions,
substitute handoff, missing materials, absent learners, feedback use, print
assembly, and cleanup. StrandPlan's arithmetic is useful, but a sum of minutes
does not establish a feasible lesson. The printing instrument distinguishes
machine geometry from physical measurement; short-edge duplex belongs to the
imposed booklet and is not a rule for every document.

The educator lens proposes complete planning bundles. The operations lens asks
for a smaller fallback packet that a teacher can use during an outage, without
an account, a new print job, or repeated data entry. Both are retained as output
options. The feedback lens proposes measuring the next learner action rather
than the length of a feedback paragraph. [EEF's feedback guidance](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/guidance-reports/feedback/)
emphasizes the quality and use of feedback and its cost to teachers; the proposal
does not transfer its findings into an effect-size claim for this application.

**Disposition D17:** evaluate complete tasks, including recovery and transfer to
paper, with a manual baseline. A faster first page that causes extra correction,
cutting, explanation, or reprinting is not established workload relief.

**Proposals:** I08–I12, I18–I25, I33, I38. **Counterposition retained:** exceptional
case handling could crowd the main interface. Make the normal path short and
the relevant contingency reachable when needed. **Revisit when:** authorized
teacher sessions show greater total preparation or recovery burden than the
manual task, or a fallback compromises the learning target.

## Session 18 — Engine trust and the lifetime of a project

**Question:** Which technical foundations deserve protection, and what proof is missing?

`ApprovedArtifact` binds an exact revision, findings, reviewer receipt, and asset
content/provenance identities. The current sink gate refuses Amber because no
production authority can issue an effective permission. The cloud adapter sends
strict registered schemas and performs separate local validation; none of this
makes generated facts true. Those seams should remain shared by every future
composition. The council proposes regression evidence for future changes rather
than replacing those controls with a new generalized assistant.

The continuity lens examines ADR-010 and the current hash-less `ProjectManifest`.
It recommends the proposed schema-2 direction: retain legacy packages, bind the
new declarative contract explicitly, and re-admit through a reviewed copy. The
classroom lens objects to repeated review cost. The response is to make exact
differences and recovery legible, without asserting a recipe identity that the
old package never recorded. A new renderer route must also address standalone
symbol continuity; updating frozen baseline bytes is not migration.

**Disposition D18:** recommend ADR-010's full design for accountable disposition,
with an explicit ADR-007 decision and a separate exact version act. This session
ratifies neither. Add rights evidence that reaches the actual sharing operation:
`SourceLens` currently issues a warning for explicit unknown rights, and the
warning itself does not block approval, save, or export. An asset provenance hash
does not resolve rights to plain excerpt text.

**Proposals:** I26–I33, I35–I37. **Counterposition retained:** additional provenance
fields can produce confident nonsense. Bind them to exact material and separate
the declaration from verification; missing evidence remains visible. **Revisit
when:** a mutation crosses an output boundary, a legacy package cannot be read,
or a versioned artifact fails its semantic or byte correspondence check.

## Session 19 — The free commons, governance, and succession

**Question:** What makes the program free in practical use and maintainable after its founder?

Code and first-party recipes are GPL-3.0-or-later. Other content is not thereby
licensed; the present documentation/content default and outside-contribution
hold prevent an honestly complete open educational commons. The council proposes
a concrete licensing decision, not a retrospective grant over material owned by
others. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) permits broad
reuse with attribution; [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
adds a compatible share-alike requirement for shared adaptations. Both summaries
warn that copyright licensing may not supply other necessary permissions.

The openness lens favors CC BY-SA 4.0 for new project-owned expressive educational
content to keep shared adaptations open. The interoperability lens favors CC BY
4.0 to reduce remix friction. **Recommendation:** prefer BY-SA for that defined
new-content class, retain BY as the explicit alternative for owner review, keep
code GPL and existing third-party licenses intact, and decide through the rights
inventory and matching contributor-assent process. This packet changes no license.

The sustainability lens proposes a stranger-build and stranger-edit exercise,
an accepted deputy role, costed maintenance, and an archive independent of a
provider account. UNESCO's [OER mandate](https://www.unesco.org/en/open-educational-resources/mandate)
connects reuse capacity, policy, inclusion, sustainability, and cooperation.
Its [teacher AI competency framework](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/ai-competency-framework-teachers)
also supports including agency, ethics, technical understanding, pedagogy, and
professional learning in training. Neither source supplies a local adoption
decision or a trained teacher.

**Disposition D19:** make openness a usable right with editable artifacts,
understandable notices, contribution routes, funding boundaries, and successors.
**Proposals:** I01–I04, I17, I22, I26, I31, I34–I40. **Counterposition retained:**
volunteer sustainability cannot be presumed; costs and actual capacity must be
recorded. **Revisit when:** a contributor cannot build, edit, localize, or share
an authorized resource without the founder or a paid service.

## Session 20 — Adversarial synthesis and proposed next commissions

The final session challenged four attractive but unsupported shortcuts: adding
every atlas idea; converting structural test counts into teaching-quality scores;
treating synthetic agreement as real council authority; and promising a permanent
commons before licensing, portable visual continuity, and succession are proven.
The resulting dispositions preserve the whole ambition through distinct tasks.

| Proposed commission | Concrete result | Dependency and stopping condition |
|---|---|---|
| C1 — Remove ambiguity in readiness | One current evidence index, exact authority choices, and explicit treatment of conflicting legacy prose (I01–I04, I34–I37) | Existing owners decide; no synthetic signature or inferred enactment |
| C2 — Make current teaching outputs complete | Comparison/checkpoint support, discussion time/decision surface, source-evidence and rights bindings, and honest communication fields (I08–I16, I26, I39) | Bounded implementation commission; recipe/version changes follow ratified identity rules and protected review |
| C3 — Make access and reuse portable | Separately reviewed language furniture, semantic reading order, editable export, and versioned visual snapshots (I17–I21, I27–I29, I32) | Exact language/AT/rights decisions and schema/version disposition as applicable |
| C4 — Prove complete teacher tasks | Reviewed preparation/recovery exercises, manual comparison, independent ratings, and physical print evidence (I05–I07, I18, I22–I25, I33, I38) | Real H0–H7 entry conditions and freeze sequence; a failed task remains a finding |
| C5 — Grow a maintainable commons | Outside contributor exercise, deputy acceptance, source archive, cost plan, selected new compositions (I30–I31, I34, I40) | Rights, contribution, maintenance capacity, H0 selection, and product-owner disposition |

These commissions are recommendations, not adopted dates or release milestones.
Repairs to a current requirement and candidate product selection must remain
distinguishable. No candidate score is fabricated; the owner can authorize a
bounded repair without claiming the real council chose a new atlas entry.

**Advisory rulings D20:** retain teacher authorship, independent evidence,
source-linked uncertainty, paper sufficiency, reversible artifacts, and open
maintenance as common design obligations. Reject surveillance, learner profiling,
automated grades/placement, AAC rearrangement, autonomous consequential messages,
and model-created safety instructions within the stated program. Keep the
Restricted candidates outside 1.x; treat Amber and protected variants as held
research/design questions. The complete map is a way to invite correction, not
an entitlement to implement its rows.

**Completion of this commission:** a synthetic master educator, eight documented
sessions, reasoned disagreements and dispositions, complete atlas and teaching
coverage, evidence assessment, and 40 concrete proposals are supplied. Perfection
of the application, future empirical effectiveness, universal cultural coverage,
real council proceedings, and release approval are not findings of this review.
Their remaining evidence is named so the ambition can be pursued honestly.
