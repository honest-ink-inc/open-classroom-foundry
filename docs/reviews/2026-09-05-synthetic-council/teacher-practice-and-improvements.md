# Teacher practice and improvement proposals

**5 September 2026 — synthetic advisory work, not an adopted curriculum or backlog.**
Read the [charter and sessions](README.md) first. These proposals are original
design judgments from the repository evidence, not observations of teachers or
learners. Each remains subject to the [implementation plan](../../implementation-plan.md),
accepted ADRs, and the [human-review sequence](../../council/bounded-commission-review-ledger.md).
No classroom material, translation, pilot instrument key, or participant record
is supplied here.

## The complete teaching cycle

A teacher should be able to enter at any useful point. A blank graph-paper press
must not demand a unit plan. For a composed lesson, however, its relationships
should remain visible: target → learner task → evidence → possible teacher response.
The response is a teacher decision, not a stored learner classification.

| Moment | Proposed teacher-facing work product | Audit question |
|---|---|---|
| Understand the setting | Generic time, material, language, access, and prior-instruction constraints | Did we ask for unnecessary learner information or infer a need? |
| Select a worthwhile target | Concept, disciplinary practice, transfer use, and source/version | Is the task aligned to what the teacher intends, rather than merely a standards label? |
| Locate prerequisites | Teacher-authored prerequisite map and short checks | Can missing evidence remain unknown rather than become a diagnosis? |
| Plan the arc | Sequence, spacing, assessment opportunities, flex points, and revisit dates | Does the calendar describe a feasible route, not a mandatory pace? |
| Prepare and anticipate | Materials, model, nonexample, likely reasoning paths, and contingency | What could a teacher have to repair at the last minute? |
| Launch and explain | Purpose, access route, concise explanation, concrete/abstract connections | Does simplification remove the concept or disclose the answer? |
| Model and rehearse | Worked example, explanation of decisions, partially faded practice | Is a different valid strategy still welcome? |
| Invite participation | Talk, writing, drawing, pointing, existing communication routes, pass and repair | Are the alternatives useful and do they preserve the target? |
| Notice understanding | A bounded check with evidence-to-next-move alternatives | What would distinguish misconception, ambiguity, and insufficient evidence? |
| Respond and extend | Small reteach, another example, additional challenge, or wait | Can the teacher reject the suggested move without recreating the artifact? |
| Give and use feedback | Evidence pointer, priority, opportunity to revise, and follow-up check | What will the learner actually do with it? |
| Support agency and transfer | Optional aids, removal/reuse choices, independent application | Is fading chosen for access and transfer rather than speed or compliance? |
| Enact and recover | Transitions, substitute script, paper fallback, absence route, cleanup | Can an unfamiliar adult use the authorized materials without confidential addenda? |
| Connect and reflect | Actionable family information, co-planning, teacher-authored revision note | Does reflection avoid accumulating response-derived or identified records? |
| Steward and continue | Editable source, exact provenance, history, archive, and repair instructions | Can another teacher or maintainer adapt the authorized work without the founder? |

These are coverage dimensions, not a prescribed universal lesson formula. Early
learning may center play, oral language, objects, and repeated routines; advanced
study may center sustained inquiry, proofs, critique, or professional practice.
The same interface must not equate more text, a faster response, or a higher
reading level with better learning.

## Every studio, with subject-specific obligations

The atlas is broad enough to raise all these questions, but it cannot establish
completeness for every culture, subject, age, language, or setting. The proposed
common contract must admit additions and legitimate practices it cannot yet model.

The [IES foundational-reading guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/21/Published)
is a scoped K–3 reference, not a license to extrapolate to all languages or ages.
The [IES instruction-and-study guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1)
motivates testing retrieval, spacing, explanation, and representation connections,
with different strength of evidence for its recommendations. Neither constitutes
an evaluation of these modules. [CAST UDL 3.0](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/)
informs the questions about agency and representation; actual access needs must
come from people, not from a synthetic persona.

## Forty implementable proposal specifications

Every item below is **PROPOSED**, not scheduled, ratified, implemented, or accepted.
Owners are accountable roles to involve, not invented appointments. “Proof” is an
acceptance condition for a future commission; none is a claim that the proof ran.
Existing requirement repair and selection of a new atlas product remain different.
No proposal admits real learner data or bypasses H0–H7.

### I01 — Choose an exact content-license and contribution route

**Need/change:** The code license does not open all educational content. Prepare
a class-by-class ownership inventory and a decision comparing BY-SA 4.0 for new
project-owned expressive content with BY 4.0 where the owner prefers remix
interoperability; preserve every existing third-party license.
**Proof:** an authorized exact-license decision, reviewed notices and matching
separate contributor assent, with rejected/unknown-rights cases still held.
**Owner/dependency:** product owner, rights steward and counsel. The applicable
license/assent entry condition precedes requesting member-authored material;
H5 later reviews the exact material and uses, not a circular prerequisite that
postpones the initial license decision until after H0. No licensing act occurs
in this report. CC's [BY](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and
[BY-SA](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) deeds explain their
different adaptation obligations and caution about other necessary permissions.

### I02 — One short decision index without duplicating authority

**Need/change:** Important terms are dispersed across long records. Add a
one-page navigation aid answering who decides, which exact record controls,
what is open, and what action would stale a decision.
**Proof:** conflicting, superseded, withdrawn and vacant-seat examples resolve
to the authoritative record or an explicit hold; no copied consent data.
**Owner/dependency:** governance custodian and maintainer; enacted controls are
inputs, not supplied by the index. Prefer links over a second constitution.

### I03 — Rehearse separate choices and withdrawal privately

**Need/change:** A consent template is not a working route. Before recruitment
or review, the custodian should rehearse independent participation, notes,
publication, credit, recording, contribution and role choices, and a withdrawal
before and after freeze using entirely fictional records.
**Proof:** changes propagate through linked dispositions; a refusal never becomes
consent; the participant can find the private contact route and its limits.
**Owner/dependency:** custodian, counsel, constituted cohort; do not promise to
erase already cloned public history or expose identities in the public receipt.

### I04 — Protect the real needs-first session from this report

**Need/change:** A 227-row synthetic map can anchor participants. Keep it away
from the initial needs-elicitation phase; let participants describe tasks,
barriers, priorities and “none of these” before showing possibilities.
**Proof:** actual H0 instrument/version and facilitator record demonstrate the
sequence; final participant-reviewed bytes freeze before feasibility or owner
selection. Synthetic recommendations remain a separate artifact.
**Owner/dependency:** real council and facilitator, after entry conditions;
this packet is not a prefilled H0 record or a recruitment message.

### I05 — Optional teaching-cycle links, not a mandatory mega-wizard

**Need/change:** Link a teacher's target, task, check and response alternatives
across approved components, while preserving a one-purpose press route.
**Proof:** a short lesson composition exposes all four links; a blank-form task
requires no unnecessary lesson text; editing a linked component invalidates only
the affected approval and composite approval, never silently reuses consent.
**Owner/dependency:** product owner, curriculum reviewers, maintainer; new
composition selection follows H0. Compare entry burden with the direct route.

### I06 — Show coverage and intellectual demand, not standards name-dropping

**Need/change:** A standards label can hide superficial alignment. Offer
teacher-authored target decomposition, prerequisite links, evidence location,
and introduce/develop/revisit/assess distinctions across a unit.
**Proof:** an independently reviewed task can be traced to its exact source
standard/version; an omitted or merely mentioned construct remains visible.
**Owner/dependency:** curriculum reviewers and teacher authors; source permissions
and I39. No automated standards-coverage certification or learner mastery score.

### I07 — Independent, stratified instructional review

**Need/change:** Structural tests accept many pedagogically empty fields.
Supplement the existing corpus with rights-cleared, stratified materials and
independent ratings for target preservation, factual grounding, access, usable
feedback and justified uncertainty.
**Proof:** predeclared criteria, complete sampling frame, disagreement records,
adjudication and held cases; report each dimension rather than a single opaque
quality number. Thirty to fifty fixtures must not become thirty copies.
**Owner/dependency:** H2 curriculum reviewers and educator council; H5 material
rights and contribution terms. Synthetic self-review is not independent evidence.

### I08 — Make Scaffold Smith's comparison and checkpoints real

**Need/change:** Required rationale strings do not demonstrate scaffolding.
Expose the original task alongside each proposed change; represent the barrier,
preserved demand, removable support, learner action and checkpoint explicitly.
**Proof:** show a counterexample that leaks the answer, one that removes the
target, and a valid alternative approach; retain teacher correction and unresolved
judgment. A checkpoint must elicit evidence, not repeat “check understanding.”
**Owner/dependency:** maintainer and H2 reviewers; versioned recipe change under
I36. Do not train the software to judge an individual learner.

### I09 — Connect examples, retrieval, feedback and transfer

**Need/change:** A stack of questions is not a learning sequence. Compose
teacher-chosen worked examples, partial fades, independent attempts, spaced
returns and explanation prompts without claiming one schedule fits everyone.
**Proof:** answer-bearing examples remain distinct from independent work; a
teacher can alter spacing and see intended knowledge/transfer coverage.
**Owner/dependency:** curriculum reviewers and maintainer; H0 for new composition,
I07 for evaluation. Test purposeful variation, not mere random rewording.

### I10 — Replace compliance proxies with agency and chosen support

**Need/change:** Scaffold Smith's task-entry default uses starting within 30
seconds as a fade criterion. Propose language and choices based on access,
transfer and learner preference; make continued use, return to support, refusal
and repair legitimate.
**Proof:** reviewers can identify no forced withdrawal of a useful aid or
speed-as-mastery claim; participation options have different usable actions.
**Owner/dependency:** H1 AAC/SLP, H2 curriculum, H4 AT and maintainer; no synthetic
replacement of an established communication system.

### I11 — Give Forumwright a time-fit and topic-decision surface

**Need/change:** Current structured questions and labels lack an explicit time
budget or sensitive-topic disposition. Add teacher-entered time, priorities,
contingency cuts, evidence purpose and a bounded topic-review note.
**Proof:** an overfull plan exposes the tradeoff; all five move families need
not be forced into every minute; a sensitive case stays held for its responsible
reviewer without automatic political or moral classification.
**Owner/dependency:** curriculum/safeguarding reviewers and maintainer; I36.
A keyword list cannot substitute for contextual judgment.

### I12 — Design feedback around the next action

**Need/change:** Couple a criterion and source-evidence pointer to a revision
opportunity, learner choice, help route and later check; separate teacher
feedback from automatically assigned grades or inferred traits.
**Proof:** independent reviewers can enact the proposed next step and identify
what evidence would change it; ungrounded feedback stays unresolved.
**Owner/dependency:** H2 and, for actual work, district-approved Amber architecture;
I35. The [EEF guidance](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/guidance-reports/feedback/)
supports examining feedback use and teacher cost, not a product effect claim.

### I13 — Bind inquiry claims to evidence and uncertainty

**Need/change:** Inquirywright's nonempty sourcing/corroboration fields are not
corroboration. Represent each supplied claim, exact source span, context,
independence relation, disagreement and permissible inference.
**Proof:** invented quotation, irrelevant corroboration and duplicated dependent
sources cannot masquerade as supported evidence; multiple defensible readings
and “not established” remain possible.
**Owner/dependency:** H2 disciplinary reviewers and maintainer; I26 excerpt rights
and I39 facts. Never manufacture a source to fill a required box.

### I14 — Typed quantitative truth where a formal model exists

**Need/change:** Reuse deterministic arithmetic for units, dimensions, scales,
rounding, bounds, uncertainty and representation correspondence, not just page
geometry. Keep assumptions visible in finance, science, mathematics and code.
**Proof:** known-answer and adversarial vectors cover unit mismatch, zero/negative
limits, rounding, domain restrictions and missing data; reference models do not
execute arbitrary teacher code.
**Owner/dependency:** disciplinary reviewer and maintainer; new engines require
H0 and I36. Correct computation alone does not validate a lesson.

### I15 — Make source fidelity and uncertainty inspectable

**Need/change:** Give capture/transcription tasks a source-to-output comparison
that distinguishes literal transcription, teacher correction and inferred
structure; never silently “repair” punctuation, numbers or ambiguous tokens.
**Proof:** unresolved tokens remain visible through review; a correction
invalidates affected approval; a layout-only change cannot alter source wording
unnoticed. Rights-cleared staged fixtures include plausible ambiguities.
**Owner/dependency:** maintainer, H2 and H4; no intake of classroom photographs
or actual work during development.

### I16 — Make family and classroom communication actionable

**Need/change:** KinDispatch's sentence-length heuristics do not prove clarity.
Require a purpose, requested action, date/deadline or explicit inapplicability,
help/contact route and teacher-confirmed source for consequential facts.
**Proof:** a missing action or ambiguous deadline is visible; semantic review can
reject a mechanically short message; literal quoted content stays literal.
**Owner/dependency:** H3 family/language reviewers, H2 and maintainer; I36.
No sending, certified translation, or completion claim is automated.

### I17 — Separate and review artifact-language furniture

**Need/change:** Localized UI and bilingual body rows do not translate English
artifact labels. Define versioned language resources for headings, notices,
dates and layout semantics separately from teacher text.
**Proof:** exact pair/script catalog and font files receive scoped H3/H4 review;
a missing resource or unsupported PDF character routes honestly to a supported
output instead of a language claim. Synthetic RTL tests remain labeled.
**Owner/dependency:** multilingual and AT seats; I27 schema implications.
No fabricated reviewed translation or universal glyph claim.

### I18 — Prove an offline task and its recovery path

**Need/change:** “Local-first” should cover opening, editing, approval, print and
recovery with an unavailable provider, lost printer or interrupted save.
**Proof:** a rights-cleared task on the declared low-end hardware completes or
fails with a recoverable explanation; retained prior bytes survive interruption;
a prepared paper fallback needs no learner device or account.
**Owner/dependency:** maintainer, H4, H6 and H7. Separate engineering fault injection
from real teacher workload. Do not change process timeouts to conceal stalls.

### I19 — Deliver genuinely editable, accessible open output

**Need/change:** HTML/print HTML and project packages are not the atlas's promised
DOCX/ODT route. Choose a bounded, versioned editable exporter with an explicit
semantic fidelity contract before advertising those formats.
**Proof:** another editor opens the file; reading order, headings, tables,
language, source text, attribution and teacher edits survive a documented
round-trip. Report unsupported constructs instead of rasterizing everything.
**Owner/dependency:** maintainer, H4/H5 and product owner; H0 selection and I36.

### I20 — Access choices must preserve purpose

**Need/change:** Offer previewable alternatives for density, size, contrast,
response area and representation, with purpose-specific descriptions. Distinguish
image identification, instruction and assessment-safe description.
**Proof:** H4 can trace meaningful order and complete the task; H2 can detect a
changed construct or answer leakage; a nonvisual route is actually usable.
**Owner/dependency:** AT, curriculum and implicated H1/H3 seats. Accessibility
templates assist authoring; they do not confer conformance or an individual plan.

### I21 — Make symbol meaning and stewardship explicit

**Need/change:** A provenance hash does not establish the meaning of a symbol.
Review each proposed asset's sense, context, variants, ambiguity, text alternative,
license and safe use; keep text/no-symbol alternatives.
**Proof:** H1 and H5 bind their separate findings to exact asset and catalog
bytes; no unreviewed substitution, established-AAC rearrangement or proprietary
redistribution enters a pack.
**Owner/dependency:** AAC/SLP and rights stewards with H3/H4 as implicated;
existing symbol candidate packet and I27/I28 for topology changes.

### I22 — Teach the teacher's judgment, not just the buttons

**Need/change:** Add short, rights-cleared exercises in inspecting drafts,
rejecting unsupported claims, choosing access routes, responding to evidence,
co-planning and recovering from failure.
**Proof:** authorized participants can explain the boundary and complete the
task; completion is not inferred from attendance or a tutorial checkbox.
**Owner/dependency:** educator council, H2/H4/H7 and district for deployment;
I01 contributor route. AI literacy should include human agency and ethics as well
as operation, consistent with [UNESCO's teacher framework](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/ai-competency-framework-teachers).

### I23 — Plan the actual classroom transitions and contingencies

**Need/change:** Connect materials preparation, setup, timing, roles, transition,
absence, substitute handoff and cleanup to the approved instructional sequence.
Keep confidential addenda outside generic project artifacts.
**Proof:** a rights-cleared tabletop rehearsal exposes missing materials, an
unfamiliar adult's unanswered question, a time overrun and a feasible fallback.
**Owner/dependency:** educator council and maintainer; H0 for new compositions,
H2/H7 for enactment evidence. Do not synthesize emergency school policy.

### I24 — Give arts, movement and CTE their own craft contracts

**Need/change:** Add discipline-specific planning fields for intent, technique,
rehearsal, critique, embodied action and material constraints instead of imposing
a worksheet model. Preserve locked school-approved safety sources.
**Proof:** relevant reviewers can demonstrate a valid creative alternative and
a rejected unsafe or purpose-changing variant; health/allergy/medical details
are never requested as generic settings.
**Owner/dependency:** disciplinary and safeguarding reviewers, implicated H1/H4;
H0 selection and I39. No generated hazardous procedure.

### I25 — Preserve learner-held self-direction

**Need/change:** Build only blank or generic goal, strategy and portfolio
structures whose completed identified record stays with the learner. Let aids,
evidence choices and reflection change without uploading a history.
**Proof:** software can print useful unfilled templates without names, scores,
diagnoses or completed responses; no analytics or re-import shortcut accumulates
them. Teachers explain the limits of any classroom process separately.
**Owner/dependency:** educator council, privacy/records reviewers; H0 selection.
This is not a digital substitute for deferred identified portfolios.

### I26 — Carry rights evidence to the exact use and sink

**Need/change:** Inquirywright explicitly allows warning-only unknown excerpt
rights; asset checks do not cover all text. Inventory teacher declarations and
verified rights separately, bind them to exact text/assets and intended use, and
propose a fail-closed public redistribution decision.
**Proof:** unknown, incompatible, permission-limited and modified cases have
operation-specific outcomes; private lawful-use questions are not settled by
an overbroad global “illegal” label.
**Owner/dependency:** rights steward/counsel, maintainer, H5; I01 and I27 for durable
fields. Design proposal, not a legal determination or implemented sink repair.

### I27 — Disposition ADR-010 without inventing legacy evidence

**Need/change:** Seek explicit human disposition of all ADR-010 clauses: preserve
hash-less schema 1, require exact schema-2 declarative-contract identity, admit
portable semantics explicitly, bind locales/assets, and re-admit reviewed copies.
**Proof:** an accountable accepted/rejected/amended record plus migration vectors
for unknown, conflicting and unregistered identities; originals remain unchanged.
**Owner/dependency:** ADR authorities, product owner and maintainer; ADR-007 and
exact engine version remain separate decisions. This report ratifies neither.

### I28 — Preserve standalone visual meaning in a new snapshot route

**Need/change:** Current portable snapshots use semantic symbol placeholders;
they are not equivalent standalone visual supports. Design a new versioned route
that packages admissible inert visuals and their provenance with accessible text.
**Proof:** disconnected, non-application viewing preserves intended symbols and
reading order; malicious references fail closed; old renderer and sample bytes
remain available and unchanged.
**Owner/dependency:** maintainer, H1/H4/H5, I27 and I36. Do not “refresh” the
ratified first-admission corpus to hide a new rendering contract.

### I29 — Approval-aware composition, history and undo

**Need/change:** A future composite must bind exact constituent revisions and
rights, not just filenames or approval-looking labels. Make change impact and
undo legible without reusing a stale authorization.
**Proof:** mutation, substitution, reorder and changed locale/asset tests either
produce fresh reviewed authority or refuse every sink; undo restores content
but does not manufacture missing approval.
**Owner/dependency:** maintainer, H2/H4 and product owner; H0 selection, I27/I36.
Reuse the existing approval seam; avoid a privileged bypass for composites.

### I30 — Keep inference optional and provider changes accountable

**Need/change:** Retain provider-neutral structured contracts and deterministic
use; evaluate a local adapter only against a named capability kit, footprint,
license, refusal, schema and egress behavior.
**Proof:** unavailable/changed providers cannot prevent opening existing Green
work; every response still undergoes local validation; a capability failure is
visible and does not fall back to unconstrained text.
**Owner/dependency:** maintainer, district and rights reviewers; accepted provider
change record. An empty local-adapter project is not a shipped local model.

### I31 — A stranger must be able to continue the program

**Need/change:** Ask an authorized outside contributor to build from the
documented locked graph and edit a bounded recipe without founder intervention;
pair this with a separately accepted deputy-maintainer role.
**Proof:** retain encountered failures and repair the instructions; record actual
scope, acceptance and succession authority privately as required; archive a
reproducible source/toolchain inventory without credentials.
**Owner/dependency:** product owner, maintainer and contributor; I01 terms.
A recruitment intention or successful CI job is not a second maintainer.

### I32 — Join mechanical access/print evidence to real instruments

**Need/change:** Use the corrected NVDA and print scripts on an exact build and
sample set, with separate routes for native PDF, browser print, Letter/A4,
grayscale, duplex, folding and unsupported characters.
**Proof:** retain actual screen-reader utterance findings and measured physical
dimensions/registration; record device, driver, settings and failure messages.
**Owner/dependency:** H4 AT, H6 print reviewers and custodian after entry terms.
Do not call generated PDF inspection a physical test or a UIA pass an NVDA pass.

### I33 — Diagnose instability without rewriting the symptom

**Need/change:** Use content-free process/console evidence to distinguish an
actual stalled child, unobserved event, test failure and external load; retain
the sightings register's open status until a cause and repair are measured.
**Proof:** an exact controlled reproduction and negative/control runs explain
the signature; retain names/messages and unchanged timeout; passing alone is
not a diagnosis.
**Owner/dependency:** maintainer; existing bounded runner and console instruments.
No classroom text or raw provider payloads in diagnostics.

### I34 — Keep current claims, histories and hosted evidence separate

**Need/change:** Close the evidence index lag with exact run/merge records and
maintain a single current-state marker. Label historical sample cohorts and
test counts by their source revisions, not as today's universal inventory.
**Proof:** ledger measurement matches every recorded conclusion; links resolve;
the current claim cannot be supported only by an older green run.
**Owner/dependency:** maintainer and product owner; append-only historical
corrections and exact source/artifact bounds. Publication remains a separate act.

### I35 — Resolve contradictory derivative-lane prose explicitly

**Need/change:** Plan §10.6 describes an approved cluster summary as Green while
the lane contract denies automatic downgrade. Request an authoritative correction
that distinguishes newly authored generic pattern descriptions from response-
derived text; inventory §10.6, atlas #60/#151 and every consumer.
**Proof:** each source-to-sink example retains its origin and legal/technical
holds; no checkbox, threshold or anonymous label grants a Green permission.
**Owner/dependency:** privacy/records authority, product owner and maintainer;
I37 and district Amber design. Until resolved, use the stricter current boundary.

### I36 — Preserve recipe identity through explicit change control

**Need/change:** Each future content, warning, schema, renderer, locale or asset
change must name which contract changes and the matching new identity, regression
suite, human re-review and migration consequence.
**Proof:** ratified C1/C2 ancestry and immutable sample baseline remain intact;
a changed contract cannot reuse a frozen identity unnoticed; render differences
are reviewed rather than normalized away.
**Owner/dependency:** maintainer, ADR authorities and every implicated seat;
existing identity rules and I27. A display-name change is not an identity migration.

### I37 — Minimize and validate the authority chain

**Need/change:** Extend synthetic adversarial cases for stale terms, expiry,
vacancy, recusal, withdrawal and partial consent while making the custodian's
private process understandable. Keep an unknown or unrepresentable case held.
**Proof:** exact-byte final records, detached manifests and later event chains
resolve only with current authenticated authority; no self-hash or identity
leak; multi-person recusal remains held until a representable revised procedure.
**Owner/dependency:** governance/records owners and maintainer; genuine enactment
and authentication remain human even when record grammar validates.

### I38 — Measure total teacher work and usefulness honestly

**Need/change:** Compare a complete authorized task with a manual baseline,
including review, correction, printing, assembly, explanation and recovery;
separate first-use learning from repeated use.
**Proof:** predeclared outcomes and failure thresholds, consenting participants,
honest exclusions and uncertainty, participant-reviewed aggregate findings;
report negative workload results rather than only time to first page.
**Owner/dependency:** real council and H7 facilitator after H0–H6; no claim of
student learning improvement from this staff task study.

### I39 — Version and review disciplinary facts, standards and safety sources

**Need/change:** Bind source version/date/jurisdiction, quoted spans, locked
facts, subject assumptions and reviewer scope. Separate contemporary rates/rules
from stable mathematics and interpretation from quotation.
**Proof:** expired standards, altered safety wording, invented historical claims,
wrong script/notation and unsupported scientific inference remain visible or
held; conflicting valid interpretations retain dissent.
**Owner/dependency:** qualified curriculum, language, rights and safeguarding
reviewers as applicable; I07/I13/I14. Software cannot ratify curriculum or policy.

### I40 — Make the open commons sustainable and repairable

**Need/change:** Couple the eventual content-license route to editable packs,
attribution, issue/patch processes, a small recurring cost inventory, maintenance
capacity, archival export and an end-of-life promise.
**Proof:** an authorized teacher can obtain, adapt and share a rights-cleared pack;
a successor can restore the program and identify obligations without a paid
provider, founder account or unexplained generated binary.
**Owner/dependency:** product owner, rights steward, maintainer and community;
I01/I19/I31. [UNESCO's OER mandate](https://www.unesco.org/en/open-educational-resources/mandate)
supports examining capacity and sustainability, not assuming volunteers exist.

## What the council declines to recommend

No “master educator” chat agent replaces bounded recipes. No learner surveillance,
automated grading/placement/discipline, inferred diagnosis or emotion, autonomous
consequential correspondence, generated safety directions, established-AAC
rearrangement, or unrestricted student-data collection is proposed. No omnibus
release, new runtime schema, license enactment, council appointment or real review
is performed by this packet. The complete candidate map is an invitation to
critique; it cannot make a human needs-first session redundant.
