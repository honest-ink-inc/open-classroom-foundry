# Individual dispositions for all 227 atlas candidates

**5 September 2026 — complete synthetic advisory map; no candidate is selected.**
Names, numbers and lane tags below are transcribed from [Atlas 2.0](../../idea-atlas.md)
at the packet's evidence baseline. The refinements are newly authored design
questions, not evidence that the described product exists or has passed review.
The [40 proposals](teacher-practice-and-improvements.md) define each I-number.

The posture column is a boundary, not a priority or verdict of readiness:

- **Current-base audit:** a bounded current starting-module/press foundation is
  known; the full atlas promise remains unproven. Repair requires its own scoped
  authorization and recipe/change review.
- **H0 option:** an unselected possibility for the real needs-first process.
  Reuse of an existing primitive does not automatically admit this product.
- **Generic only; protected variants held:** the G→R entry has a possible generic
  design, but individual/protected functionality is not admitted.
- **Amber held:** actual response-derived use has no current effective production
  permission. A separately authored Green exercise does not downgrade actual work.
- **Outside 1.x:** Restricted or identified longitudinal functionality stays out.
- A trailing **specialist review** preserves the atlas's H marker; the actual
  accountable seat and scope must be determined, not supplied by a fictional lens.

For G/A rows the Green option is independently teacher-authored material; the
Amber route remains held. For G→R rows neither the arrow nor a generic default
authorizes a Restricted variant. All new selections require frozen H0 evidence,
separate feasibility and product-owner disposition. Existing protected, rights,
district, accessibility and release holds remain in force.

## Coverage is not adoption

All 227 source entries have a distinct row and a concrete question. This makes
omissions and disagreement inspectable; it does not claim 227 implementations,
227 approvals, universal pedagogy, actual human deliberation, or a release queue.
The real council may identify a need outside the atlas or choose no new product.
