## Mandatory order

Each row begins only after the preceding row's procedurally valid record has
been finalized, participant-reviewed, and bound afterward by a detached freeze
manifest. H1 specifically requires a real H0 council record made under enacted
terms and matter-specific quorum after recusals; an unranked consultation is
not an H0 record. “Frozen” means the final record bytes are never edited, their
version, byte length, and SHA-256 are recorded in a separate completed manifest,
and every downstream record binds both the record SHA-256 and the manifest
SHA-256. A manifest never contains its own digest. Freezing does not mean the
finding is favorable, and it does not erase a dissent or hold.

The validator can prove only that the current supplied bytes and their links are
hash-consistent. It cannot prove that a previously completed record was never
rewritten together with its descendants. Before any downstream use or decision,
including a dependent human session, feasibility assessment, product-owner
disposition, adoption, implementation, ADR, district or stage-gate decision,
correspondence, public-evidence filing, republication, or release consideration,
a human Git-history audit must identify the first complete version of each
claimed frozen record and confirm that its bytes have not changed. Without that
history evidence, the record is not treated as frozen even when current-tree
hashes agree.

Immutability does not make an old disposition eternally current. Immediately
before each downstream use or decision, the preparer must repeat the upstream-
chain audit through the exact candidate commit and obtain a fresh private-
custodian attestation. The audit binds the frozen root and manifest plus the
complete append-only event chain and one current effective disposition for each
upstream record. A later public event, private event, or relevant source/control
change makes that audit stale before the next use. A withdrawal, restriction,
unresolved correction, ambiguous branch, missing link, or conflicting current
disposition is an explicit **HOLD**. No downstream reviewer, product owner, or
typist may select an older revision to evade an event already known in the
current tree, repository history, or private-custodian record.

Their quorum field is exactly `NOT APPLICABLE — H7 participant activity; no
council recommendation`, and their disputed-recusal field is exactly `NOT
APPLICABLE — H7 participant activity; no council recusal decision`.

A versioned H7 aggregate root then binds the complete ordered set of session-
record and session-manifest paths, versions, byte lengths, and SHA-256 values,
plus every linked correction or withdrawal disposition. Every contributor whose
material remains in the proposed aggregate reviews its exact bytes and
separately re-confirms public-record publication consent for those bytes before
the aggregate closes. Changed bytes restart that review and consent cycle.
The aggregate and H7 root-binding records use their exact aggregate/root N/A
values for council-seat authority, quorum, and disputed recusal; neither value
may appear in the later council-review matter.

The later council evidence review is a distinct H7 council matter and record.
It carries seated/non-vacant, present, practicing-educator, and recused council
counts, matter-specific quorum arithmetic, consensus or tally, objections, and
protected-seat holds. Non-member educator participants appear only by
de-identified count and authenticated bounded role; they never count toward
council quorum and never vote. The H7 final root binds both the frozen aggregate
and the frozen council-review record, and its one detached H7 root manifest
binds that exact complete set. The eight-root release rule therefore remains
eight H0–H7 final roots and eight root manifests while retaining the additional
H7 supporting records needed to prove each session.

## Append-only event and chain-resolution rule

Every append-only event records a unique ID and version, event type, effective
UTC instant, affected frozen root ID/path/digest, predecessor event ID and digest
or `NONE — first event`, de-identified affected scope, accountable authority and
private-custodian attestation reference, current-use consequence, outstanding
holds, and—when the event is public—its repository path and byte length. Private
identity, exact participant wording, contact, consent, and payment data never
enter the public record or a public hash.

The pre-use chain audit enumerates the full event graph discovered in the
current tree and repository history, reconciles it with a fresh opaque private-
custodian attestation, and names one current effective disposition. It is not
enough to repeat the manifest's “none at freeze” value: that field is a
historical at-freeze statement and is never edited. Every downstream record and
its detached manifest bind the chain-audit ID, UTC instant, exact audit path,
version, byte length, SHA-256, and candidate repository revision. Until a
versioned resolver exists, completeness and authority remain a human check;
uncertainty fails closed.

## Release boundary

H0–H7 are evidence dependencies, not release authorization. Only after all eight
procedurally valid final records and their eight detached freeze manifests are
closed and their separate digests are bound downstream may a release-scope
audit even be prepared. Immediately before each proposed outward act, that
audit must bind the exact proposed source revision; the current effective H0
final record, detached manifest, feasibility record, and product-owner
disposition chains; every H1–H7 final root and manifest; all H7 supporting
records; every relied-upon ADR, district, stage-gate, rights, accessibility,
print, correspondence, and public-evidence authority record; every public
append-only event; and a fresh private-custodian attestation. It must find no
unresolved correction, withdrawal, supersession, ambiguity, conflict, or hold.
Every other hold and applicable Gate 0–5 row must be dispositioned by its
accountable authority, the project-schema/version decision separately ratified,
and the pre-publication history check complete.

Any later public or private event, control change, evidence change, or source
change relevant to that outward act stales the audit and requires a new one.
Selecting an older commit cannot hide a known later event. Only after those
conditions are freshly met may the typist consider republishing or begin
release rites; this ledger performs neither act.
An unranked consultation is never one of those eight records. This ledger never
supplies that typist act, and an earlier deployment supplies no standing
permission.
