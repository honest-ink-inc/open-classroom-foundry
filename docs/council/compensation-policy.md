## Terms the 29 August document presented as policy

1. **No one pays to advise.** Materials, printing, and builds are provided; sessions fit inside 30–60 minutes; no travel is asked for. A council seat must never cost its holder anything but the time they choose to give.
2. **PROPOSED REPLACEMENT — NOT ENACTED: credit is opt-in and prospective.** No participant would be named publicly without a separate affirmative credit choice. A participant could change that choice for future releases and current editable surfaces. Because this repository and its release history are public and copied by others, neither withdrawal nor a changed credit choice can promise to erase earlier Git history or already-distributed artifacts. The current record could be corrected or carry a prospective withdrawal of credit without pretending every historical copy can be recalled. This proposal does not silently amend the 29 August credit wording; no new public credit should be made until the conflict is resolved through the exact typist-approved proposal and valid first-cohort enactment route above.
3. **Compensation never buys a finding or continued participation.** Under the proposed correction, when the lawful-payment prerequisites below have been reverified, an authorized disbursement route is operational, the participant has separately elected compensation, and the full disclosed session rate fits within that participant's remaining quarterly cap, the full rate attaches once the scheduled session begins—not to completion or a conclusion. Stopping or withdrawing does not forfeit it. A separately declined compensation election attaches no payment and is never made retroactive. An advisor who accepts compensation and stops, says the module fails, or says it ships receives the same pre-session disclosed amount.
4. **HISTORICAL PRIORITY WORDING — HELD, NOT OPERATIVE.** The 29 August text said that, if any budget existed, AAC/SLP and assistive-technology seats would be paid “first and best.” It did not define an aggregate budget, a reservation order, or a rate different from the other specialist rate, so it cannot operate as a scheduling or payment rule. The proposed `OCF-COMPENSATION-v1` replacement gives every covered specialist capacity the same stated rate and gives no seat priority over an amount already owed. Scarcity may guide future fundraising and recruitment planning, but it does not reorder scheduled sessions or authorized debts. Any later aggregate-budget priority requires its own exact, enacted control; this proposal does not invent one.
5. **FACTUAL LICENSE CORRECTION AND HOLD — NOT A NEW LICENSE DECISION.** The repository licenses code and first-party recipes under GPL-3.0-or-later, but its separate license for documentation and original printable content has not been chosen. The former sentence that purported to place fixtures, findings, and council materials under GPL-3.0-or-later had no recorded license grant and is withdrawn. Council participation, compensation, note-taking consent, participant review, and public credit therefore grant no GPL or other contribution license. Member-authored fixtures, exact quotations, translations, illustrations, and other materials remain outside the repository and distribution artifacts unless the project declares the applicable content license and the author separately assents to those exact contribution terms. No IP assignment has been approved; whether Honest Ink, Inc. becomes copyright steward remains a separate open decision.

## The honest constraint, and the phase structure it forces

The repository record last verified on 30 August 2026 says the proposed entity
had applied for Maryland incorporation and had not yet filed with the IRS. No
authorized entity disbursement route is recorded. Entity status, EIN, banking,
tax, employment, and payment-law prerequisites must be reverified with counsel
and an accountant before any promise or first disbursement.

No participant session begins until that dated reverification is recorded in the
private ledger and an authorized disbursement route is operational. Before each
session, the private ledger, keyed to one compensated natural person rather than
to a seat or capacity, records the participant's separate compensation election,
the exact applicable rate, calendar-quarter assignment, quarter-to-date paid and
committed amounts across every covered session or consultation, the remaining
cap, and an opaque district-time/no-double-payment attestation. The cap uses UTC calendar
quarters: January–March, April–June, July–September, and October–December. A
scheduled compensated session creates a full-rate reservation in the quarter of
its scheduled UTC start. Do not schedule it when that reservation would exceed
the remaining cap. If it is cancelled before beginning, the ledger appends the
cancellation and releases the reservation; if rescheduled, the ledger appends
the old and new UTC starts, releases the old reservation, books the new quarter,
and repeats the cap check. The actual start instant controls the final quarter:
if delay would cross a quarter boundary, rebook and recheck before beginning.
Once begun, the reservation becomes paid or owed and is never released because
the participant stops or withdraws. A participant may separately decline
compensation without losing the opportunity to participate; that session is
recorded as zero, creates no deferred or retroactive payment, and is never used
to pressure a finding or continued participation.

- **Phase 1 — preparation and enactment only; no participant session:** complete the exact corrective proposal and valid first-cohort enactment; make the replacement credit language and the private custodian, access, retention, correction, and withdrawal-right terms operative; reverify the lawful-payment prerequisites; and establish the authorized disbursement route and private compensation ledger. This phase creates no session, promise, deferred honorarium, or retroactive balance.
- **Phase 2 — participant sessions only after every Phase 1 prerequisite is complete:** proposed honoraria follow the complete H0–H7 classification below, including a session the compensated person elects to stop, capped at **$300 per compensated natural person per UTC calendar quarter across all covered sessions and consultations**, regardless of membership or number of capacities. The full applicable rate must fit under that person's remaining cap before a compensated session is scheduled; no partial rate silently cures an over-cap session. A separately declined session is zero and non-retroactive.
- **District-time pathway:** if any covered participant service occurs on compensated district or other employer time, that natural person's project honorarium pauses for the overlapping session — no double payment. Before scheduling, the private ledger records `NOT ON COMPENSATED EMPLOYER TIME` or `HONORARIUM PAUSED — compensated employer time`, with an opaque basis/authority reference and no employment detail in the public record.

For this proposal, **specialist** means an expressly constituted AAC/SLP,
accessibility/AT, multilingual/family-communication, privacy/legal/records,
safeguarding, curriculum/content, rights/OER, or district capacity. **Educator**
means a general educator, mechanical print/educator reviewer, or teacher-pilot
participant acting only in that capacity. The accepted constituted seat
capacity controls a member's rate; an accepted and authenticated bounded
non-member H7 participant role controls that non-member's rate. One person
serving in more
than one capacity during the same scheduled session receives one non-stacked
rate—the highest applicable rate disclosed before scheduling—subject to the
same quarterly cap. The typist's facilitating or product-owner acts, and
counsel/accountant verification acts, are not participant-session roles and
carry no honorarium under this policy; any separate professional engagement
requires its own authorized agreement and is outside this policy. These
classifications are part of the corrective proposal and have no effect unless
the exact policy version is validly enacted.
