**Proposed control identity:** `OCF-COUNCIL-TERMS-v1`. This label is not an
enactment or a content hash.

The pre-enactment bar does not forbid the minimum one-to-one administration
needed to offer and constitute the first cohort. Before storing anything, the
proposed private custodian must give the candidate the proposed purpose, fields,
access boundary, retention/deletion date, correction route, and withdrawal
route and obtain affirmative administrative-record consent. Only contact and
scheduling details, the exact seat/scope offer, accept/decline/withdraw status,
qualification/authentication basis, and exact terms assent may be retained.
That exception permits no research session, needs evidence, observation,
recording, quotation, authored contribution, compensation promise, peer
disclosure, or public record. A decline or withdrawal stops further collection;
if the seat is not constituted, the custodian deletes the candidate record by
the disclosed date except for a minimum no-contact marker or record that law
requires to be retained, and documents that narrow disposition privately.

This draft gives the first cohort concrete language to amend or adopt. It does
not appoint anyone, authenticate a participant, decide who is qualified for a
seat, choose a content license, authorize publication, or transfer copyright.
The standing rule in [GOVERNANCE.md](../../GOVERNANCE.md) remains controlling:
no role may waive another role's critical gate.

## 1. Membership, seats, and terms

1. Before enactment, every invitee is a candidate, not a member, and no
   candidate seat counts toward quorum. An invitation, pilot session,
   contribution, attendance, or bare statement of acceptance is not an
   appointment to the council.
2. For the initial cohort, the product owner is the proposed appointing
   authority. Each seat is constituted only in the enactment record after the
   product owner has issued an offer naming the exact seat and bounded scope,
   the candidate has accepted that exact offer and the enacted terms, and the
   product owner has recorded the qualification/authentication basis. The
   private record holds identity and supporting evidence; the public record
   holds appointing authority, stable de-identified person/seat authority
   references, seat, scope, effective UTC instant, exclusive-expiry UTC instant,
   person-count, qualification-basis category, and private-custodian reference.
3. After enactment, a replacement or additional seat requires a recommendation
   under the enacted general decision rule and a written product-owner offer,
   followed by the same offer, acceptance, qualification, and public/private
   record split, except for the narrow subquorum reconstitution rule in section
   6. Without quorum, that rule, or resolved qualification, the seat remains
   vacant. Appointment cannot make an unqualified reviewer qualified,
   manufacture independence, or clear a protected gate by itself. The enactment
   record must privately identify a non-member independent second maintainer or
   counsel, plus a backup, for the limited conflict and continuity checks in
   sections 5 and 6; the public record uses stable opaque authority IDs unless
   each person separately elects public credit. Each must separately accept the
   exact bounded role and these terms. The enactment record states each opaque
   authority ID's acceptance disposition, independence/conflict finding,
   qualification-basis category, authority basis, scope, effective UTC instant,
   exclusive-expiry UTC instant, and private custodian reference; it names the
   person publicly only after a separate affirmative public-credit choice. If
   either acceptance and record is missing, those exceptions are unavailable.
4. The typist keeps identities, contact details, acceptance records, and
   scheduling privately. A public record names seats and counts by default.
   Public identity requires a separate, affirmative credit choice.
5. The proposed term is one calendar year. Each authority record uses an
   effective UTC instant and an exclusive-expiry UTC instant. The expiry is the
   same month, day, and UTC time one year after effectiveness; a 29 February
   effective instant expires at the same UTC time on 28 February of the next
   year. Authority is valid only at or after the effective instant and strictly
   before the exclusive expiry. Recheck it before every matter decision. If a
   term would expire during a session, adjourn before expiry; later presence or
   a vote cannot cure it. Renewal requires, before expiry, a fresh
   written offer from the non-conflicted product owner (or the exact
   conflict-substitute authority already valid under section 5) naming the
   seat, bounded scope, and new term; the member's fresh acceptance of that
   offer and the then-current exact operating-terms and compensation controls;
   revalidation of the qualification/authentication basis; and a new
   public/private authority record under item 2. Otherwise the term expires and
   the seat becomes vacant. Resignation is effective whenever the member states
   it; no reason is required. A term does not continue silently.
6. A person holding more than one capacity counts as one person for quorum and
   voting. Every capacity must be disclosed. One person's presence does not
   satisfy two independent evidence requirements unless the governing
   requirement expressly permits that combination.
7. General educator members may report their practice. Specialist members may
   make findings only within the seat they accepted and the evidence they
   actually reviewed. Anyone may raise a concern outside their seat, but the
   record marks it as an advisory concern awaiting the accountable seat.
8. A non-member H7 educator participant is not appointed to the council. Before
   that person's activity, the product owner or the exact bounded
   participant-role authenticator named in the enactment record privately
   records the participant-role offer and acceptance, session scope,
   qualification/authentication-basis category, supporting-evidence and
   custodian reference, and two separate conflict checks. The authenticator must
   be non-conflicted for that role. An authenticator conflict makes the exact
   authentication status **AUTHENTICATOR CONFLICT — HELD** unless the named
   alternate already granted that bounded authentication authority in the
   enactment record acts and records the same evidence; that alternate cures
   only the authenticator gap. Separately, record the candidate participant's
   de-identified material conflict categories and limitations. A participant
   conflict that undermines the bounded role's independence, safety, or evidence
   question makes the exact status **PARTICIPANT SCOPE CONFLICT — HELD**; the
   person does not begin that activity and their observations do not enter H7
   evidence. A non-conflicted authenticator or alternate cannot cure that
   participant conflict. A conflict found not to undermine the bounded role is
   retained as **PARTICIPANT CONFLICT — LIMITED**, with the written limitation
   applied to evidence and no inference beyond it. An unresolved disputed
   conflict remains held. These terms create no ad hoc substitute. The public
   record carries only opaque authority/participant references, categories,
   scope, count, limitations, and any held status. This route creates no seat,
   quorum, vote, product authority, or protected-seat finding.

## 2. Scope and protected-seat authority

The council recommends; it does not ship, publish, ratify an ADR, approve a
district deployment, or make the product owner's priority disposition.

- Council quorum never supplies a missing specialist review.
- A protected-seat review applies only to the named artifact, revision,
  evidence, and question that person actually examined.
- Presence, silence, abstention, elapsed time, another member's expertise, a
  facilitator's summary, and automated evidence never mean protected-seat
  assent.
- If AAC/SLP, accessibility/AT, multilingual/family communication,
  privacy/legal/records, safeguarding, curriculum, rights/OER, or district
  territory is implicated and its accountable seat is vacant, absent, or
  recused, that territory remains **NOT REVIEWED — HELD**.
- A protected seat may hold work within its territory. It does not thereby
  select the overall product priority or speak for another protected seat.
- No proxy vote or proxy specialist approval is recognized by this draft.

## 3. Meetings and notice

The proposed cadence is one scheduled meeting each month while a pilot or
release-evidence cycle is active and at least one meeting each quarter
otherwise. Additional meetings may be requested by two seated members or by the
product owner. Except for a meeting limited to urgent safeguarding or record
preservation, members receive the proposed agenda and review materials at least
seven calendar days beforehand.

The facilitator is non-voting unless separately seated as a member. The
facilitator records boundaries, time, and process; they do not score needs,
rewrite findings, resolve a protected question, or cast a tie-breaking vote.

## 4. Proposed quorum and recommendation rule

These rules have no effect until enacted.

1. General quorum is a majority of all currently seated, non-vacant members,
   never fewer than three natural persons, including at least two members seated
   as practicing educators.
2. Quorum is checked again for each matter after recusals. A recused member does
   not count toward that matter's quorum or decision.
3. The council first seeks consensus through a read-back of the exact proposed
   recommendation. If unresolved objections remain, the facilitator records
   them and a vote may be taken.
4. A recommendation passes only with more than half of the eligible,
   non-recused members present voting in favor. Abstentions and silence are not
   votes in favor. A tie or failure to reach that majority means **NO COUNCIL
   RECOMMENDATION**.
5. The tally, abstentions, unresolved objections, and dissent are recorded.
   Dissent is preserved; it is not rewritten into apparent consensus.
6. Any applicable protected-seat hold survives a general recommendation.
7. A meeting without quorum may gather, clarify, and map needs. Its record is an
   unranked consultation and must not contain a council ordering.

After enactment, an amendment to these terms requires section 4 matter-specific
quorum after recusals and affirmative votes from at least
`ceiling(2 × eligible non-recused seated natural persons / 3)`, where the
eligible denominator includes every then-seated, non-vacant, non-recused natural
person whether present or absent. The record states that denominator, eligible
and practicing-educator persons present, and yes/no/abstain counts. No quorum or
an affirmative count below that ceiling means no amendment. The resulting
governance record follows GOVERNANCE.md, and no amendment retroactively changes
the rule applied to an earlier decision.

## 5. Conflicts and recusal

Before substantive discussion, every participant discloses any material
financial, employment, supervisory, authorship, vendor, family, or personal
interest in the question. Ordinary session compensation under a separately
corrected and then-operative policy is not by itself a conflict because it is
independent of the finding.

- The public record names the conflict category and affected matter without
  naming the person by default; the private roster retains the identity.
- A conflicted member may answer bounded factual questions but does not frame,
  rank, recommend, or vote on that matter.
- For a disputed recusal, calculate general quorum while excluding the affected
  member. If quorum does not remain, the recusal dispute and underlying matter
  are **HOLD**, and the record states that quorum was not met and no decision was
  made. If quorum remains, the eligible non-conflicted members first seek
  consensus after an exact read-back. Only if that consensus fails may they vote
  on one stated disposition; more than half of the eligible non-conflicted
  members present must vote for it. Record the denominator, tally, abstentions,
  exact read-back confirmation, and rationale. The affected member and
  facilitator do not authorize the result. A tie or failure to reach that
  majority holds the dispute and underlying matter. A held dispute cannot be
  represented as resolved, and its underlying matter cannot be recommended; both
  remain explicitly held, and the affected member takes no part, until a later
  properly quorate decision resolves the dispute.
- A material product-owner conflict in enacting or amending these terms holds
  that governance act. The independent reviewer cannot replace the
  product-owner action GOVERNANCE.md requires. A material product-owner conflict
  in offering a seat or recording a candidate's qualification basis is
  disclosed before that act; the product owner recuses, and the seat remains
  vacant unless the enactment record's named, non-conflicted independent second
  maintainer or counsel accepts the exact bounded appointing role, acts within
  it, and records written reasons. If neither the named reviewer nor backup is
  available and non-conflicted, the appointment is held. A product-owner
  conflict in a later priority disposition is recorded there by category and
  written finding. A material conflict makes the exact status
  **PRODUCT-OWNER DISPOSITION HELD** and permits no adoption. These terms create
  no substitute priority authority; only a separately ratified governance/ADR
  route could create one. The product owner cannot alter a frozen council record
  to cure any conflict.
- Adverse findings, principled dissent, accessibility needs, withdrawal, or
  declining public credit are never grounds for removal.

## 6. Vacancies, removal, and continuity

A resignation, expired term, or removal makes the seat vacant. Stopping one
session or withdrawing one future use/collection permission is not itself a
resignation. A member who declines an activity's required permission does not
participate, count as present, or vote in that activity, but remains in the
seated, non-vacant matter-quorum denominator unless recused or the seat becomes
vacant under this section. A member who ends all future council participation
thereby resigns immediately, without needing a reason or special word, and the
records steward appends the effective UTC instant and vacancy record. Work
governed by that seat stays held until a duly constituted successor performs
the required review; earlier evidence remains attributed to the revision
actually reviewed.

If a resignation, seat-term expiry, removal, or a required non-member H7 role's
closure or authority expiry takes effect while any council matter,
protected-seat review, H7 session, or other bounded H0–H7 activity is underway,
the activity stops immediately and any council matter adjourns without a
decision. The records steward first appends the effective UTC instant and
applicable vacancy or role-closure event. No decision, quorum finding,
review-completion claim, or
completed-review freeze may use the earlier roster/vacancy cutoff or
denominator. A detached manifest may preserve an honest stopped/held record,
but it does not satisfy the interrupted review dependency. A reconvened activity
or matter requires a fresh roster/vacancy or participant-role cutoff taken after
that event,
rechecks every exercised authority across the complete new activity or matter
interval, and recomputes quorum and every denominator from that fresh cutoff. A
stale denominator is invalid.

If vacancies make general quorum mathematically impossible, the ordinary
quorum-backed recommendation for a replacement is unavailable. A narrowly
bounded reconstitution may then fill only enough already-defined vacant seats
to restore the minimum general quorum, without changing a seat's scope or
creating an additional seat. It requires written concurrence from both the
non-conflicted product owner and the non-conflicted independent continuity
reviewer named in the enactment record, the ordinary offer, acceptance, and
qualification evidence, notice to every remaining seated member, and a public
record of the reason and person-count. A conflict, disputed qualification,
missing reviewer, or disagreement holds the appointment. Reconstitution does
not decide a priority, amend these terms, or clear any protected review; once
quorum is restored, all ordinary replacement and additional-seat rules resume.

Removal for cause is proposed only for inability to perform the accepted role,
material breach of session boundaries, or conduct that makes safe participation
impossible. It requires written notice, a reasonable opportunity to respond,
section 4 matter-specific quorum after the affected member and every other
conflicted member are recused, and affirmative votes from at least
`ceiling(2 × eligible non-recused seated natural persons / 3)`. The record states
that eligible denominator, eligible and practicing-educator persons present,
yes/no/abstain counts, and the product owner's concurrence. The affected member
may respond but does not frame or vote on removal. If quorum is absent, the vote
misses the ceiling, or the product owner is materially conflicted, removal is
held; an independent reviewer has no substitute-removal authority under this
proposal. An urgent safety concern may stop and adjourn a session and hold later
participation pending this process, but it does not silently vacate the seat.
Removal may not erase dissent, retroactively alter an earlier denominator, or
clear a protected gate.

Members would select a convener and a records steward, with a named backup for
each, after these terms are enacted. If the convener, facilitator, product
owner, or maintainer becomes unavailable, the backup may preserve records and
schedule an unranked consultation only. Product-owner, maintainer, signing,
publication, district, and protected-seat authority do not transfer by absence,
convenience, or automation. Any durable succession of those roles follows the
governance and ADR process.

## 7. Decision and evidence records

Every public council record is de-identified by default and distinguishes:

1. an unranked consultation;
2. a council recommendation made under enacted quorum and decision rules;
3. a separate protected-seat review;
4. a participant-reviewed final meeting record and its separate freeze manifest;
5. a later feasibility assessment;
6. a product-owner disposition; and
7. an ADR or district decision, where required.

None of those records substitutes for another. A recommendation record includes
the session date, repository/build revision, seats present and absent, the exact
operating-terms version, matter-specific quorum, conflict categories and
recusals, the exact recommendation or absence of one, tally or consensus
read-back, dissent, abstentions, outstanding holds, participant corrections,
and the final record version.

Freezing closes the participant-reviewed record bytes against later editing.
Only afterward does a separate manifest record that final record's path,
version, byte length, and SHA-256; the manifest contains no self-hash. Every
downstream record binds both the final-record and completed-manifest digests and
a fresh, complete append-only-chain audit made at the exact downstream
repository revision. Neither file is edited after closure. This is not a
promise that public Git history can be erased. Later corrections, withdrawals,
credit changes, or supersessions are new append-only linked records rather than
silent rewrites of historical bytes. Before downstream use, the chain audit
must enumerate every public event by path/version/length/SHA-256, reconcile
private events through opaque custodian attestations, name the current effective
disposition, and carry any unresolved or conflicting event as a HOLD.

## 8. Participation, credit, and contribution rights

Participation consent, private/de-identified note-collection consent,
public-record publication consent, recording consent, within-cohort identity
and affiliation disclosure, public credit, compensation election, content
contribution, maintainer appointment, and copyright stewardship are separate
choices. Agreement to one never supplies another. The withdrawal right and
route are explained and acknowledged separately; withdrawal is a right, not an
opt-in choice or a condition of participation.

Before any group session, each participant privately chooses the exact identity
and affiliation information, if any, that may be disclosed to the named cohort
recipients. Contact details are never shared by default. Every recipient first
acknowledges that the information is session-confidential and may not be reused
for outside contact or disclosure. A decline is honored through a seat-only or
pseudonymous format where workable, or an offered one-to-one consultation that
remains outside H0–H7 and supplies no quorum or dependency. No introduction is
automatic, and public-credit consent cannot supply this peer-disclosure choice.

The project has not selected its separate license for documentation and
original printable content. Until it does and a member separately assents to
the applicable contribution terms, member-authored fixtures, exact quotations,
translations, illustrations, and other materials remain outside the repository
and distribution artifacts. A participant-reviewed, de-identified factual
session record is not permission to reuse their authored material for another
purpose.

Withdrawal from a stated use ends that future use and collection. Stopping one
session does not by itself resign a council seat. Ending all future council
participation is an immediate resignation and vacancy under section 6; ending a
non-member H7 role closes that bounded role. Before a record is committed or
otherwise published, the participant review step is the point to remove or
correct their material. Once a public record has entered immutable Git history
or has been copied by others, erasure cannot be promised; the current record can
carry a correction, withdrawal marker, or prospective credit change.
Stopping or withdrawing never forfeits an otherwise applicable session
honorarium: under any corrected and operative policy, after lawful-payment
prerequisites, an authorized route, the separate compensation election, and the
pre-session full-rate/cap check are recorded, the full disclosed amount attaches
once the scheduled session begins, independent of completion or conclusion. A
separate compensation decline attaches no amount and is not retroactive.
