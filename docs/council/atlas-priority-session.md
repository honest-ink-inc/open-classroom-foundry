Explain the withdrawal right and route and record acknowledgement separately—withdrawal is not an opt-in choice. - A member may speak from their own practice, not for an unfilled seat. Silence or absence means **not reviewed**, never assent.

- A candidate touching AAC, accessibility/AT, multilingual/family communication, district, privacy/legal/records, safeguarding, curriculum, or rights territory stays held for every applicable seat that was absent. One role cannot waive another role's critical gate.

## Sixty-minute needs-first agenda

1. **Boundaries and read-back — 5 minutes.** Read the non-negotiable boundaries aloud. Confirm that the software, not the educator, is under review. Confirm every separate participation/use choice, compensation election, and the independently explained withdrawal right and route; resolve conflict/recusal status before the affected matter.
2. **Silent need capture — 10 minutes.** Each member completes up to three private need cards without seeing candidate names. One card describes one recurring production burden or missing support. The private custodian keeps the originals; the recorder prepares only de-identified factual paraphrases for the proposed public H0 record.
3. **Clarify, do not solve — 10 minutes.** Members ask only enough to understand frequency, current workaround, classroom consequence, and what a useful artifact would let the teacher or learner do.
4. **Cluster needs — 8 minutes.** Group duplicates while preserving materially different contexts and dissent. Return original cards to the private custodian; do not copy or quote them into the repository.
5. **Open the atlas — 10 minutes.** Map each surviving need to an existing entry, an already-built capability, or a genuinely new composition. “No match” is valid and must remain visible.
6. **Test value and boundaries — 10 minutes.** For each leading need, record the first useful proof, unacceptable failure, data lane, and possibly implicated seats. An absent applicable seat creates a hold.
7. **Recommend and read back — 7 minutes.** Record an ordered recommendation only if the present members actually make one. Read the wording, dissent, uncertainty, and holds back to them before closing.

Once the detached manifest is complete, neither the final H0 record nor its manifest is edited. A correction, withdrawal marker, prospective credit change, or supersession is a separate append-only linked record. It cannot silently rewrite the historical bytes or claim erasure from Git history or distributed copies.
