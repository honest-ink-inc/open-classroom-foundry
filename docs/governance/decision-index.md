Before relying on a record, follow its exact source/version and the
[append-only audit](append-only-chain-audit-template.md) through the candidate
revision and fresh private-custodian attestation. A missing, conflicting,
superseded, withdrawn or unrepresentable authority is **HOLD**, not a best guess.

Four navigation checks illustrate the rule, without inventing a completed audit:

- **Conflicting claim:** §10.6's former Green-summary sentence yields to §4;
  the dated correction preserves the old error and authorizes no Amber sink.
- **Superseded record:** follow the linked event chain; an old favorable root
  does not override the current effective disposition. Missing successor: HOLD.
- **Withdrawal:** stop the affected use and ask the private custodian to resolve
  the linked disposition. Do not edit a frozen predecessor or promise clone erasure.
- **Vacant or expired seat:** stop the affected matter and recompute only after
  fresh authenticated authority; another role or automation cannot supply assent.

The [implementation register](accepted-improvement-register.md) tracks work;
it does not replace any controlling record above.
