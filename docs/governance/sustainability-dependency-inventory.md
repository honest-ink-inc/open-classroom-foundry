## Decision record needed before commitments

For each commitment, the responsible human must supply its exact scope, named
accepted owner and backup, evidence date, measured cost/capacity and currency or
explicit nonmonetary basis, funding/cap authority, renewal or expiry trigger,
failure and exit route, and a reviewed disposition. Keep account credentials,
participant records, payment identifiers and private contact records outside the
public repository. A vacant owner or unverified amount stays unresolved rather
than becoming zero cost or infinite capacity.

I40 is not closed by this table. Its commons, editable-output, independent-build,
teacher-use and succession dependencies remain in the full
[implementation register](accepted-improvement-register.md). Actual EIN,
1023-EZ, signing approval and outward filings remain deferred; their absence
does not excuse feasible engineering or factual preparation.
